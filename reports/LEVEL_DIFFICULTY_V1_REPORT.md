# Level Difficulty V1 - opening boards, limited-shots schedule, target scaling

## Problem

Levels could be cleared by repeatedly pushing gems up a single line. The root
cause was structural, not tuning: every generated level started on an **empty
table** (`starting_board` was always `[]`), and layouts were pure permutations
of the launcher sequence. With nothing to aim around, horizontal position never
mattered, so one column was as good as any other.

Two secondary problems fell out of the same shape:

- Limited-shots levels did not appear until level 10, long after the mechanic
  stopped being novel.
- Every level asked for exactly one gem of each target tier, so once board
  density capped out the objective stopped changing and later levels became
  indistinguishable from one another.

## Changes

### Seeded opening boards

Levels from 2 onward open with a pre-placed cluster. Rows are staggered so the
centre lane is never clear, which is what forces horizontal decisions.

- Row count grows with level and caps at `STARTING_BOARD_MAX_ROWS` (4), so the
  table never starts crowded enough to deny the launcher a landing spot.
- Row growth slowed from every 2 levels to every 3, because the old rate hit the
  cap by level 8 and the board stopped changing.
- Density instead keeps rising through **gap count**: rows leave two gaps below
  level 12 and one gap from level 12 on. The gap is rolled once per row; an
  earlier version rolled inside the column loop, which left some rows sealed and
  others missing several gems.
- Only spawnable tiers are placed, so every seeded gem can still be merged into.
- The lowest row sits `STARTING_BOARD_DANGER_MARGIN` (250px) above the danger
  line, so an opening board never starts the player near a loss.
- Layouts are seeded from the level seed, so a retry presents the same puzzle
  rather than becoming a reroll.

### Limited-shots schedule

- First limited-shots level moved from 10 to **4**, immediately after the three
  levels that teach the merge loop.
- Recurs every 3 levels (`LIMITED_SHOTS_CYCLE`), so it reads as a returning
  variant rather than a phase, and never runs back to back.
- Shots tighten from `SHOT_LIMIT_INTRO` (40) by 2 per limited level down to
  `SHOT_LIMIT_FLOOR`, raised from 26 to **30** once target quantities began
  scaling.

### Target quantity scaling

`target_quantity()` scales the two lower target tiers with level, capped at 3x
tier 6 and 2x tier 7. The top tier is always 1: it is the longest merge chain in
the level, so repeating it would multiply the hardest work rather than deepen the
objective.

## Resulting curve

Generated by `scripts/dev/print_level_curve.gd` (developer aid; nothing loads it
at runtime):

| level | type | shots | opening gems | targets | total | shots/gem |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | normal | - | 0 | 1/1/1 | 3 | - |
| 4 | LIMITED | 40 | 3 | 1/1/1 | 3 | 13.3 |
| 7 | LIMITED | 38 | 5 | 2/1/1 | 4 | 9.5 |
| 10 | LIMITED | 36 | 8 | 2/1/1 | 4 | 9.0 |
| 13 | LIMITED | 34 | 14 | 2/2/1 | 5 | 6.8 |
| 16 | LIMITED | 32 | 14 | 3/2/1 | 6 | 5.3 |
| 19+ | LIMITED | 30 | 14 | 3/2/1 | 6 | 5.0 |

Pressure rises continuously (13.3 -> 5.0 shots per required gem) and then holds,
rather than shrinking forever.

## Solvability

Hard levels may strongly encourage powers, but no level may be mathematically
unwinnable without them. `run_level_difficulty_v1_tests` enforces this for every
limited level from 4 to 60:

- the shot limit respects the floor,
- the limit is at least one full launcher cycle, so the player cannot run out
  before the sequence has offered the tiers a target needs,
- the limit exceeds `total_target_quantity(level) * 4`,
- `total_target_quantity()` matches the generated `target_sequence`, so a future
  edit to quantities cannot drift away from the floor that protects it,
- every seeded gem is a spawnable tier, and
- every seeded row leaves a gap.

## Validation

- All 18 Godot regression suites pass, with zero script errors. The script-error
  count is checked explicitly because a GDScript runtime error aborts the
  enclosing test function and can otherwise let a suite report PASS with its
  remaining assertions skipped.
- `tests/capture_powers_v1.gd` re-rendered; `opening-level-01/04/08/14.png`
  reviewed for density, route, and danger-line clearance.
- Headless import clean.

## Not covered

The solvability guarantee above is a **bound**, not a proof of play: it shows
the shot budget comfortably exceeds the target requirement, but it does not
simulate an actual playthrough. Confirming that the tightest levels (19+, at 5.0
shots per required gem) are comfortable rather than merely possible needs device
playtesting, which no automated check here substitutes for.
